    How this works

    Race data is checked every morning at 7:00 Bangkok time against organiser sites and race listings. Races whose registration has closed or sold out are removed automatically. Prices marked ≈ come from the previous edition. Always confirm on the official site before paying.

    Relevance combines a race's size, international profile, certification (World Athletics, AIMS) and how much of a destination race it is.
